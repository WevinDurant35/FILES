Results and Discussion
The strategy adopted for synthesis of B(1→2)-linked disaccharide composed of two 4-deoxy-D-arabinohexopyranose units was to prepare a glycosylation agent promising the high B-stereoselection. Trichloroacetimidate 4 turned out to be the proper reagent because of two reasons: 1) the bulkiness of 2-O-tosyl group has been reported to exert the complete stereochemical control for the attack on the opposite side [6], 2) a-O-trichloroacetimidates with non-participating C-2OR group proved to form stereoselectively B(1→2)-
-trans glycosides [7]. Coupling of trichloroacetimidate 4 with arabino
derivative 6 should form B-trans disaccharide 7, providing after conversion of xylo unit into the arabino isomer the desired B-cis linked disaccharide 10.
